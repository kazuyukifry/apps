# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Spread is a free iOS app developed by KAZUYUKI FURUYA. This policy explains how the app handles your photos and data.

## General Policy
Spread processes your photos exclusively within your device. Your photos and data are never transmitted to any external servers.

## Access to Photos
This app uses the standard iOS Photo Picker. Therefore, it does not require permission to read your entire photo library. Only the specific photos you select will be shared with the app.

## Saving Photos
This app uses "Add Only" permission solely when saving exported images to your photo library.

## Data Collection
This app does not collect data for usage analysis, tracking, or advertising. We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information.

## Third-Party Disclosure
Since no data is collected, no information is shared with third parties. Once images are transferred to other apps (such as Instagram) via the share function, the privacy policy of the respective recipient app will apply.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
