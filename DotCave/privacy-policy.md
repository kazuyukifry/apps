# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

DotCave is a free iOS app developed by KAZUYUKI FURUYA. This policy explains how the app handles your data.

## General Policy
This app does not use the network. Your data is never transmitted to any external servers.

## Data Stored on Your Device
The app stores your high score and settings (such as drawing and display options) only on your device. They are deleted when you delete the app.

## Data Collection
This app does not collect data for usage analysis, tracking, or advertising. We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information. This app contains no ads.

## Third-Party Disclosure
Since no data is collected, no information is shared with third parties.

## Contact Form
We accept inquiries through Google Forms. Anything you send through the form is used only to respond to your inquiry. Google's privacy policy also applies to information handled by the form.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

## Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the [contact form](https://forms.gle/VrZ5P2RSM6YwHuEJ9).
